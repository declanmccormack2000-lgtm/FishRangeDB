Introduction

FishRangeDB brings together published records of where fish were observed, how much was caught and how long individual fish measured. It holds {{species}} species. Its purpose is to show how the recorded range and body size of each species have changed over time.

Records show where and when fish were sampled. They are not estimates of abundance or native range. Maps can be read against sampling effort, which we record for each 1° grid cell and year.

Data Sources

Occurrence:
Ocean Biodiversity Information System (OBIS), bulk snapshot, CC BY-NC 4.0.
Catch:
FAO FishStat, Global capture production, release 2026.1.0, CC BY 4.0.
Body length:
OBIS measurement records; ICES Database of Trawl Surveys (DATRAS), CC BY 4.0.
Taxonomy:
World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS); NSF NEON fish taxon list.
Coastlines:
Natural Earth, public domain.

Disclaimer

We make no warranty that records are free of errors. Sampling is uneven across regions and years, and the earliest record of a species is not its original range. Catch is reported by country and FAO fishing area, not by coordinates.

Names are not yet reconciled across all sources, so one species may appear under more than one name. Users must attribute each source and follow its licence. The OBIS snapshot is for non-commercial use.

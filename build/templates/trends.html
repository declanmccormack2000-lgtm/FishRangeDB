Method

Each point is the mean length in one survey and year. Years and means are centred within each survey, so a change in which surveys exist cannot create a trend. An estimate needs at least five years across ten years of repeated surveys and 100 measured fish.

A result describes the fish that were sampled. Fishing pressure, gear, season or age composition can each cause it, and it is not evidence of evolutionary change.

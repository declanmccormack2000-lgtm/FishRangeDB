Welcome to FishRangeDB

An archive of fish occurrence, catch and body-length records, 1743 to 2026.

Introduction

FishRangeDB brings together published records of where fish were observed, how much was caught and how long individual fish measured. It holds 14,026 species. Its purpose is to show how the recorded range and body size of each species have changed over time.

Records show where and when fish were sampled. They are not estimates of abundance or native range. Maps can be read against sampling effort, which we record for each 1° grid cell and year.

    Size Trends

    We estimate change in mean body length within each survey, so that a survey is only compared with itself. There are 1,160 estimates covering 1,053 species: 121 show a clear decrease, 81 a clear increase and 958 no clear change. The remaining species do not yet have enough data.

    Disclaimer

    We make no warranty that records are free of errors. Sampling is uneven across regions and years, and the earliest record of a species is not its original range. Catch is reported by country and FAO fishing area, not by coordinates.

    Names are not yet reconciled across all sources, so one species may appear under more than one name. Users must attribute each source and follow its licence. The OBIS snapshot is for non-commercial use.

    Japanese text is a translation; scientific names, dataset titles and download files stay in English.
